THE CHALLENGE

Problem Statement & Rural Healthcare Crisis

Why traditional invasive screening fails frontline rural communities

Silent Epidemic

Over 57% of pregnant women and 67% of children (6-59 months) in India suffer from anemia (NFHS-5), causing maternal mortality, cognitive impairment, and chronic fatigue.

Invasive Test Friction

Current gold-standard screening requires venous/capillary blood draws, expensive single-use microcuvettes, cold-chain reagents, biohazard disposal, and painful finger-pricks that cause high patient refusal.

The Last-Mile Barrier

Frontline ASHA workers operate in remote villages with zero cellular connectivity and budget Android smartphones. Centralized diagnostic labs are hours away.

THE SOLUTION

RaktDrishti: Non-Invasive Optical Screening

Turning ordinary smartphones into intelligent point-of-care triage aids

Core Innovation Pillars

  • Zero Blood Draw: Point-and-shoot camera capture of lower eyelid (conjunctiva), fingernails, and palm.
  • Low-Cost Calibration Card: Print-at-home 12-patch reference card normalizes lighting & skin tones.
  • On-Device TFLite ML: Near real-time inference (45ms) without requiring cloud servers.
  • Multi-Site Optical Fusion: Fuses mucosal erythema, capillary perfusion, and palmar pallor.

Strict Medical Safety Guardrails

  • Triage Aid, NOT Diagnosis: Estimates standardized risk tiers (NORMAL, MILD, MODERATE, SEVERE).
  • Mandatory Referral Flow: Automatically generates hospital referral slips for confirmatory CBC tests.
  • No False Comfort: Explicitly advises patients that low-risk results do not replace clinical exam.

Offline Synchronization Engine

  • Client UUIDs: Non-colliding entity identifiers generated client-side.
  • Sync Queue Statuses: PENDING ➔ SYNCING ➔ SYNCED ➔ FAILED.
  • Automatic Draining: Connectivity listeners batch-post queued records to /api/v1/sync upon reconnection.
MACHINE LEARNING & OPTICS

Optical Biomarkers & Color Normalization

Eliminating ambient illuminant shift and skin-tone bias

12-Patch Calibration

Computes 3x3 Color Correction Matrix (CCM) using least-squares to map raw sensor RGB to standard D65 CIELAB space.

Optical Biomarkers

  • Conjunctiva: Erythema Index $EI = \log(R) - \log(G)$ and CIELAB $a^*$.
  • Nail Beds: Capillary bed redness $R/(R+G+B)$.
  • Palm: Flexion crease contrast.

Fusion Formula & Score

$\text{Score} = \frac{\sum s_i \cdot q_i \cdot c_i}{\sum q_i \cdot c_i}$

Benchmark: 98.5% Accuracy, 100% Sensitivity (0 False Negatives).

PROBLEM SOLVING

Key Challenges Faced & Engineering Solutions

Overcoming real-world technical and clinical deployment hurdles

Challenge 1: Lighting & Melanin Bias

Problem: Ambient clinic lighting ranges from 2700K tungsten to 5500K direct sunlight, distorting erythema metrics across skin tones.

Solution: Standardized 12-patch reference card with real-time white-balance normalization and CIELAB $a^*$ chrominance extraction.

Challenge 2: Intermittent Offline Village Access

Problem: ASHA workers cannot depend on cell towers during door-to-door screenings.

Solution: Complete SQLite offline database mirror with client-side UUID generation and auto-draining sync queue.
